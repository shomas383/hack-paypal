# Chapter mentions in Chapter 99 note text treated as context (HTS 2026 Rev. 21)

For review (2026-10-11). Since commit a208579 the mentions index (`src/domain/estimator/mentions.ts`) does not let a chapter named in **note text** name a line (`lineOnly`): such mentions are kept as context, and chapter ranges in note text are "broad". A chapter in a **heading's own text** still names its lines (e.g. 9903.41.10, "provided for in chapter 64"). Below: every note subdivision with a chapter mention (148), the sentence it is in (cut at 260 characters), and why it is context. Generated from `pdftotext -enc UTF-8 -layout` (xpdf 4.06) of the Rev 21 Chapter 99 PDF.

**Flagged for review:** category X (a real chapter-level scope, in exemption lists) and S (a real chapter-level scope, in trade-agreement subchapters). Neither can add duty to an estimate: X only exempts from note 33/38 duties the estimator doesn't compute (lines those notes list are refused), and S belongs to special programs, which are not applied.
